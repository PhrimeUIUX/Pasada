Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 02/02/2026

Pasada (“we”, “our”, “app”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email, phone number, only if you choose to register
  • Pickup Location: Used only when you manually set your pickup point on the map
  • Usage Data: Map interactions and app usage to improve app experience

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To show nearby drivers and allow “Get Directions” to them
  • To process confirmed pickup requests initiated by users
  • To improve the app experience
  • For security and support

3. Sharing Your Information

  • Confirmed pickup location is shared only with nearby drivers if initiated by the user
  • Personal information such as name, email, or phone is not shared with drivers outside the app

4. Location Usage

  • Users manually select their pickup location on the map
  • The app does not request device GPS or location permission
  • Location is only used for confirmed pickups and map directions

5. Data Retention

  • Pickup location and usage data are temporary and tied to user-initiated sessions
  • Account information is retained only as long as the account exists

6. Security

We implement standard practices to protect user data.

7. Your Rights

  • You can review, update, or delete your account information at any time
  • See the “Account Deletion” page for instructions
