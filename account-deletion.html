Account Deletion

You can delete your Ka Pasada account at any time directly from the app by following the steps below.

  1. Open the app → Menu → Delete My Account
  2. Enter your password
  3. Confirm deletion
  4. Your account information, including name, email, phone number, and pickup history, will be permanently removed
  5. This action cannot be undone
Notes
  • Guests who have not registered do not have an account to delete
  • Temporary session data is automatically cleared

If you experience any issues deleting your account, contact support@kapasada.com
